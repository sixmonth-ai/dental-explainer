# Primary teeth, third molars, arch layouts and numbering charts

Facts: `references/dental-facts.md` §15. Read it before planning.

## Building a figure

Facts: `dental-facts.md` §15 (with §1, §2, §5). Kit: `DK.toothPreset(51–85)`, `DK.arch`, `DK.toothNumbers`, `DK.dentitionAt`, `DK.impaction`.
- **Numbering chart / whole arch / occlusal view:** copy `examples/numbering-chart.js`. `DK.arch({ jaw, dentition, thirdMolars })` lays the teeth out with contacts touching, correct chirality and the patient's right at −x; `placement: 'chart'` for the upper arch above the lower; numbers as sprites (`DK.toothNumbers`), one group per system, buttons to switch. Camera from above with a small `phi` and a looser `view.margin`. Subtitle must say which side is the patient's right.
- **Primary or mixed dentition:** copy `examples/mixed-dentition-8y.js`. `DK.arch({ dentition: 'mixed', age, side, form: 'straight', successors: true })` picks the teeth from the AAPD chronology (present primary teeth carry `resorb`, successors `develop`) and places the successors in the bone; X-ray fades the bone. For the 20 primary teeth use `dentition: 'primary'` with the chart layout. A single primary tooth: `DK.tooth(DK.toothPreset(75, { resorb }))`; it has thin enamel, a large pulp with high mesial horns, no root trunk and flared roots by itself: don't scale a permanent tooth down.
- **Third molars and impaction:** copy `examples/impacted-38.js`. `DK.toothPreset(38)` is a real third molar (short, fused, distally inclined roots; say "variable" in the subtitle). `DK.impaction({ fdi: 38, neighbour: 37, angulation, depth })` poses it by Winter's angulation and Pell & Gregory depth; drive `angle` and `depth` with `fig.param` so buttons and the story animate them, and show the inferior alveolar canal in the cut.
- Watch for: Universal letters A–T (not numbers) for primary teeth; primary molars are replaced by premolars; left teeth are mirror images (the arch does it; a lone left tooth needs `scale.x = −1`); ≤ 6 leader labels per frame, so number sprites, not labels, for a chart.

## Kit API

| call | does |
|---|---|
| `DK.toothPreset(fdi, {resorb, develop})` | Now accepts **FDI 51–85** (primary; `spec.primary = true`, `spec.successor` = the permanent FDI) and real presets for **18, 28, 38, 48** (`spec.variable = true`, `approx` false). Primary specs carry thin even enamel (`spec.dej`), a large pulp with high mesial horns (`spec.chamber`), cervical constriction, no root trunk and flared molar roots. `resorb: 0..1` shortens and blunts the roots (physiological resorption; 1 = about to exfoliate). `develop: 0..1` makes a developing permanent tooth: complete crown, root formed to that fraction with a wide open apex. Build with `DK.tooth(DK.toothPreset(75, { resorb: 0.5 }))`. `DK.PRIMARY_PRESETS` holds the ten named primary specs. |
| `DK.toothName(fdi)`, `DK.toothNumber(fdi, 'fdi' \| 'universal' \| 'palmer')` | "Primary mandibular left second molar"; Universal `1–32` / `A–T`, Palmer with the quadrant bracket (`6┘` upper right, `└6` upper left, `6┐` lower right, `┌6` lower left; `A–E` primary). `DK.isPrimary`, `DK.successorOf(54) → 14`, `DK.predecessorOf(14) → 54`. |
| `DK.dentitionAt(age, {jaw, thirdMolars})` → `{present, developing}` | The teeth of one jaw at that age from the AAPD chronology (`DK.CHRONOLOGY`, range midpoints): `present` = erupted teeth (primary ones with `resorb`), `developing` = crown-complete permanent teeth with `develop` and `under` (the primary tooth they replace). |
| `DK.arch({jaw, dentition, age, thirdMolars, side, teeth, placement, form, width, depth, spacing, successors, omit, crownsOnly, toothOpts, build})` → `{group, teeth, successors, width, depth, curve, slots}` | **Places teeth along the arch.** `jaw: 'maxilla' \| 'mandible'`, `dentition: 'permanent' \| 'primary' \| 'mixed'` (+ `age`), `thirdMolars`, `side: 'left' \| 'right'` for one quadrant, or an explicit `teeth: [fdi…]` (placed at their natural slots). **Arch frame:** midline x = 0, patient's right at −x, anterior +z, crowns up, every CEJ at y = 0; each tooth's +x points mesially along the arch and +z buccally, contacts touch, and **left-quadrant teeth come out mirrored** (`scale.x < 0`: the correct chirality; a single left tooth outside the arch needs `scale.x = −1` too). Arch form: the beta function (Braun 1998) with width `W` (outer width at the distal molars, defaults per dentition) and depth `D` solved so the perimeter equals the sum of the tooth widths; `form: 'straight'` lays the teeth on a line (use with `DK.bone` blocks). `placement`: `'natural'` (as a mandible), `'jaw'` (maxilla mirrored crown-down for the mouth), `'chart'` (maxilla crown-up with the incisors toward −z, so a camera on +z above sees a chart: upper arch at the top, patient's right on the viewer's left). `successors: true` (mixed) builds the developing teeth: premolar crowns between the flared primary molar roots, canine and incisors lingual and apical, second molars distal to the first just under the crest. `teeth[i]` = `{fdi, tooth, spec, slot: {c:[x,z], t, n, side}, resorb}`. `omit: [fdi…]` leaves those teeth out (note: tooth **numbers**, unlike `DK.tooth`'s `omit`, which takes layer names). `crownsOnly: true` builds enamel crowns only (charts from above). `toothOpts: fdi => opts` passes options to `DK.toothPreset`; `build: (fdi, spec, slot) => ({group, layers, …})` replaces `DK.tooth` for each tooth. |
| `DK.toothNumbers(arch, {system, color, halo, height, lift})` → group | One camera-facing number sprite per tooth of the arch (lifted above the cusps, in the arch's frame). Make one group per system and toggle `visible`. Colour from the theme: `fig.dark ? '#F5F3EE' : '#0A0A08'`, halo = the stage colour. `DK.textSprite(text, {height, color, halo, weight})` is the underlying helper (for "R" / "L" markers etc.). |
| `DK.impaction({fdi: 38, neighbour: 37, angulation, angle, depth, gap})` → `{group, tooth, spec, set(), angle, depthMm, winter, pellGregory}` | An impacted third molar posed against its second molar: **Winter's angulation** (`'vertical' \| 'mesioangular' \| 'horizontal' \| 'distoangular'`, or `angle` in °, + = crown tilted mesially; bands ±10°, 11–79°, 80–100°, −11 to −79°) and **Pell & Gregory depth** (`'A' \| 'B' \| 'C'`, or mm below the neighbour's occlusal plane). `group` is a pivot at the third molar's cervical centre, placed distal to the neighbour **in the neighbour's frame** (neighbour at the origin, CEJ y = 0, +x mesial), so add it next to `DK.tooth(37)` at the origin. `set({angle, depth})` re-poses it: drive it with `fig.param` for buttons and stories. Maxillary (18, 28) handled (mirrored inside the pivot). |

Pitfalls:
- **Occlusal views:** the auto-fit assumes y is the screen's vertical. A chart seen from above runs its depth up the screen, so loosen `view.margin` for the interactive page (about 1.0–1.3) but tighten it for an image (about 0.9: image frames already fit looser), and keep `phi` small (0.16). Pass `crownsOnly: true` to `DK.arch` for any chart: from above, the far arch's roots otherwise show under its crowns.
- Many teeth = many parts. Register the **arch group** as the regular part (it frames the camera) and each tooth as an `inPlace` part with `label: false` for hover; `fig.part(toothGroup)` without `inPlace` would re-parent it out of the arch.
- For a mixed dentition with a straight bone block, size the block from `arch.halfLength` and leave room for the second-molar crypt behind the first molar (its crown is about 10 mm wide).
- `DK.impaction` sets the pose, not the bone: give the figure a bone block whose crest is at the neighbour's CEJ − 1.8 and the inferior alveolar canal about 15–18 mm below the crest, then show the cut (`removeToward [0,0,1]`).
- Third molars vary: say so in the subtitle. Primary teeth in a figure: name the exfoliation age in hover text, not the eruption age.
