# Fixed prosthetics: bridges, veneers, inlays and onlays

Facts: `references/dental-facts.md` §18. Read it before planning.

## Building a figure

Numbers from dental-facts.md §18 (connector sizes by material, pontic designs, veneer depths, inlay/onlay rules); name the material in the subtitle. Kit functions: "Kit API" below.
- **Bridge** ("bridge vs implant", "what holds a bridge"): copy `examples/bridge-46.js`. Abutment roots with `DK.prep` stumps, `DK.bridge` as **one exploding part** (retainers, pontic, connectors), a resorbed ridge (bone and gingiva scallop dipping under the pontic), a story that ends on the whole bridge in a three-quarter buccal view (abutments, pontic, connectors, ridge), a bucco-lingual cut **through the pontic** as a view button for the pontic base vs ridge, panel buttons that swap `DK.pontic` designs. Label the connector with its computed mm². Default pontic: modified ridge lap (posterior mandible: sanitary is also correct). Never give the pontic a root or "attach" it to the gum.
- **Veneer** ("what is a veneer", "veneer vs crown"): copy `examples/veneer-11.js` (a procedure, crown up, labio-lingual cut). `DK.veneerPrep` morphs **only the labial face** (depth by third) and then the incisal edge; `DK.veneer` seats on it. The teaching shot is the cut: the enamel left under the prep. Keep the lingual surface untouched and say "not a crown" in the hover text.
- **Inlay / onlay**: copy `examples/onlay-36.js`. `DK.inlayPrep` (isthmus, boxes, `cusps` for an onlay) + `DK.inlay`; explode the restoration up; cut **bucco-lingually through the central fossa** so the isthmus depth, the reduced cusp and the intact cusp read together; an `Occlusal` view for the outline. No bevels on ceramic.
- Maryland (resin-bonded) and cantilever bridges: build with `DK.bridge` (a cantilever is a pontic at one end only); a bonded wing is not in the kit yet: draw it as a thin lathe/box on the palatal surface and say it is schematic.

## Kit API

Every prep here is carved on the crown's own surface lattice, so a restoration made with the **same numbers** shares the prep's vertices and seats on it exactly (like `DK.prep` + `DK.restorationCrown`). Units mm, tooth frame (CEJ at y = 0, +x mesial, +z buccal/labial). Material names: `'zirconia' | 'lithium disilicate' | 'ceramic' | 'metal-ceramic' | 'gold' | 'composite'`.

| call | returns |
|---|---|
| `DK.veneerPrep(fdiOrSpec, {cervical:0.3, middle:0.5, incisal:0.7, edge:1.5, type:'window'|'butt'|'overlap', wrap:1.0})` → mesh | **Veneer preparation you can animate**: the tooth's enamel shell (outer surface over the DEJ) whose **labial face only** eases into the prep. `m.set(labial, incisal)` (each 0..1): labial depth by third (cervical → incisal), then the incisal edge (`butt`: flat edge reduced by `edge` mm; `overlap`: plus `wrap` mm onto the palatal; `window`: no edge, the reduction fades 1 mm short of it). Margin: a light chamfer ~0.4 mm above the CEJ. Add it to `DK.tooth(fdi, {omit:['enamel']}).group` (dentin body, root, pulp); section group: enamel cap, before the dentin. In a labio-lingual cut the enamel left under the prep shows that it stays in enamel. Does not touch `DK.prepMorph`. See `examples/veneer-11.js`. |
| `DK.veneer(fdiOrSpec, sameOpts, material)` → mesh | The veneer: a closed shell between the intact labial surface and the finished prep, so it is 0.3–0.7 mm thick and wraps the edge for `butt` / `overlap`. Slide it in from +z (`position.z`). Section-ready (its own group before the tooth). |
| `DK.inlayPrep(fdiOrSpec, {isthmus:2.0, depth:1.8, boxes:['mesial','distal'], boxWidth:0.4, boxFloor:1.0, axial:0.28, cusps:[], reduction:1.5})` → group | **Inlay / onlay preparation** on a posterior tooth: a group of the carved enamel shell (`.enamel`) and the carved dentin body (`.dentin`). The occlusal isthmus (`isthmus` mm wide, floor `depth` mm below the central fossa) runs into a proximal box per side in `boxes` (gingival floor `boxFloor` mm above the CEJ, its axial wall `axial` × the half MD width in from the proximal surface, half-width `boxWidth` × the BL half-width); walls diverge ~8°, no bevels. `cusps: ['buccal']` / `['lingual']` makes it an **onlay**: those cusps are lowered by `reduction` mm. `grp.set(k)` eases the cavity and cusp reduction in (0..1) for a procedure. Add `DK.tooth(fdi, {omit:['enamel','dentin']}).group` for the roots and pulp. Section groups: `.enamel`, then `.dentin` with the roots, then the pulp. |
| `DK.inlay(fdiOrSpec, sameCav, material)` → mesh | The inlay or onlay: the solid between the original occlusal surface and the cavity (and over the reduced cusps). Explode it up (+y). Named `'Inlay'` or `'Onlay'` by `cusps`. |
| `DK.pontic(fdiOrSpec, {design:'modified ridge lap'|'ovate'|'sanitary'|'conical', ridgeY: number | (z => y), clearance:3, depth:1.2, contact:0.35}, material)` → mesh | The missing tooth's crown over a tissue-side base: modified ridge lap (contact on the buccal slope of the ridge at `contact` × the buccal half-width, lingual convex and clear), ovate (egg `depth` mm into the ridge), sanitary (`clearance` mm above it), conical. `ridgeY` is the ridge top under the pontic (give a function of z to follow a domed ridge). `mesh.contact` = [x, y, z] of the tissue contact. Closed solid, section-ready. |
| `DK.connector(x0, x1, yc, zc, h, w, material)` → mesh | A rounded prism along x between two units, `h` (occluso-gingival) × `w` (bucco-lingual) mm. `mesh.size = {h, w, area}`. |
| `DK.CONNECTORS[material][region]` | Minimum connector `{h, w, area}` by material and region (`'anterior'|'posterior'`): zirconia 9 / 12 mm², lithium disilicate 16 mm², metal-ceramic 7.5 mm², gold 6.25 mm² (dental-facts.md §18). |
| `DK.bridge({units:[{fdi|spec, x, role:'abutment'|'pontic', prep:{axial, occlusal, margin}}], material, region, connector:{h, w}, pontic:{…}, connectorY, connectorZ})` → group | A fixed partial denture as one piece: a hollow **retainer** (`DK.restorationCrown` with the unit's `prep` numbers) on every abutment, a `DK.pontic` on every pontic unit, and a `DK.connector` between neighbours (size from `DK.CONNECTORS` unless `connector` is given; centre at `connectorY`, default 55% of the shorter crown; the ends sink 0.6 mm into each unit). `units` are sorted by x. `.retainers`, `.pontics`, `.connectors`, `.units` (each with `.mesh`). Seat it on `DK.prep` / `DK.prepMorph` stumps built with the same `prep` numbers at the same x; explode it as one part (`explode: [0, 12, 0]`); one section group for the whole bridge. See `examples/bridge-46.js`. |

Pitfalls:
- The bridge's cut face is the pontic's full cross-section (it is monolithic); with the camera on the removed side it hides the units behind it. Ceramic caps are a touch darker than the body (`0xe4e0d6`) so a cut reads as a cut.
- `DK.bridge` sorts units by x: `retainers[0]` is the most distal one. Find a unit by its x (`bridge.units.find(u => u.x === X45).mesh`) before labelling it.
- Keep the veneer prep inside the enamel: with the kit's enamel (~0.3 mm at 1 mm above the CEJ, ~1 mm on the labial face), the default 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.7 mm depths do. Deeper cervical depths invert the shell locally; the dentin cap paints over it in section, but say "into dentin" in the label if you do it on purpose.
- Cavity walls follow the lattice (~0.3 mm cells): outlines are crisp on the cut face and slightly stepped in plan. Keep `isthmus` ≥ 1.5 mm.
