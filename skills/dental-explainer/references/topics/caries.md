# Caries and direct restorations

Facts: `references/dental-facts.md` §16. Read it before planning.

## Building a figure

Numbers and stages: dental-facts.md §16 (ICDAS/ICCMS, lesion shapes, ICCC 2016 selective removal, Black's classes, prep dimensions, amalgam facts, the mistakes list).
- **Caries progression** ("what is a cavity", "how decay reaches the pulp", ICDAS stages): copy `examples/caries-46.js`. One `DK.cariesLesion` driven by a `stage` param, one step per stage, mesio-distal cut through the lesion axis, hero shot on the lesion's path to the pulp. Occlusal lesions sit at the central pit (`site:'occlusal'`); proximal ones under the contact (`site:'mesial'`, `at` ≈ 0.45 × crown length). Say "stages follow ICDAS" in the subtitle. A white spot is chalky and the surface stays intact until stage 2; never draw early caries as a black hole.
- **A filling** (Class I or II, composite or amalgam, sealant): copy `examples/class-ii-composite-25.js`. `DK.cavityPrep` (the void, animated with `set`) and `DK.filling` share one `PREP` object so the restoration seats exactly; the lesion is set to a dentin stage and `removed: 1` leaves the affected dentin as "firm dentin left" (selective removal, ICCC 2016). A Class II needs the neighbour in contact, a `DK.matrixBand` and a `DK.wedge`; composite goes in ≤ 2 mm increments (`fill.slice`). Amalgam: `DK.filling(..., 'amalgam')`, one increment, say it is not bonded. Sealant: `DK.sealant(tooth)` on a sound or ICDAS 1–2 fissure.
- Watch for: the lesion must stay inside the crown (`size` ≤ 0.15 × MD); label anchors via `lesion.point()`; the prep void goes after the pulp in the section groups and is hidden once the last increment is flush; burs park out of view between steps.

## Kit API

All of these probe the real tooth meshes (ray casts in the tooth frame, once at build), so lesions sit inside the layers and preparations open exactly on the crown surface. Every solid is closed and section-ready. Materials added to `DK.mat`: `chalk` (white-spot body), `cariesSoft` (infected dentin), `cariesFirm` (affected dentin), `reactionary`, `cavity` (the void of a cavity or prep), `amalgam`, `gic`, `sealant`, `wood`; colours in `DK.COLORS` under the same names.

| call | returns |
|---|---|
| `DK.cariesLesion(tooth, {site:'occlusal'|'mesial'|'distal'|'buccal'|'lingual', at, size, stage, reactionary:true})` → group | A lesion added to `tooth.group`. `site:'occlusal'` is a pit-and-fissure lesion at `at: [xn, zn]` (normalised crown coordinates, default the central pit): narrow at the fissure, widening to the DEJ. The other sites are smooth-surface lesions at height `at` (mm above the CEJ, default 0.42 × crown length, just under the contact): broad at the surface, apex at the DEJ. The dentin lesion is a cone with its base on the DEJ in two zones (infected, affected); a reactionary-dentin lens grows on the pulp wall from stage ≈ 2.5. `size` is the lesion's half-width in mm (default 0.11 × MD width). **`g.set(stage, {removed})`** drives it, `stage` 0 → 5 (`DK.CARIES_STAGES`: sound, white spot, enamel breakdown, dentin cavity, deep, pulp exposure; fractions interpolate); `removed: 1` takes the infected zone and the cavity away (selective removal, the affected zone stays). `g.meshes = {enamel, affected, infected, reactionary, cavity}` for labels and section groups; `g.axis = {surface, dej, pulp, enamel, dentin}` (distances along the lesion axis, mm); `g.point(depth, r, k)` → tooth-frame point `depth` mm below the surface, `r` mm off the axis (`k` = column 0..23 around it, 0 = mesial/occlusal, 12 = opposite); `g.size`; `g.info(stage)` → the stage row. |
| `DK.CARIES_STAGES` | `[{stage, label, icdas, iccms, note}]` for the six stages. |
| `DK.cavityPrep(tooth, {class:1|2, side:'mesial'|'distal', center:[x,z], length, isthmus, depth, floorY, taper, box:{gingivalY, width, widthOcclusal, axial}, over, material})` → group | The prepared cavity as a closed solid: an occlusal step (rounded box along the central groove, flat pulpal floor `depth` mm below the DEJ, default 0.3) and, for Class II, a proximal box from the marginal ridge to `gingivalY` with its axial wall `axial` mm inside the DEJ, diverging slightly gingivally. In the default `cavity` material it is drawn as a hole: only its inner walls render (back faces, no depth test, `DK.asHole`), so the recess shows through the enamel from outside and nothing stands proud of the crown; give it a section group **after the pulp** with `color: DK.holeCap(fig)` (the theme’s recessed warm ink, distinct from filling material), then the filling after it. Anything that can stand in front of a hole (neighbour tooth, burs) gets `DK.inFront(obj)` (renderOrder 2); bands, wedges and fillings already have it. `p.set(kStep, kBox)` morphs each part from nothing to the full outline (bur steps). `p.dims = {floorY, isthmus, length, center, gingivalY, axialX, boxWidth}` for labels and bur paths. `p.step`, `p.box` are the meshes. `p.slice(from, to)` clamps the solid to a y range. `prep.front('step'|'box', burRadius)` → `[x,y,z]` (tooth-local) is where the cavity is growing right now: lerp the bur tip onto it while `set` grows the cavity, so the bur is always cutting the edge it opens (see `onStep` / `onBox` in the example). |
| `DK.filling(tooth, prepOpts, 'composite'|'amalgam'|'gic')` → group | The same solid with `over: 0`, flush with the surface, so it seats exactly in `DK.cavityPrep(tooth, prepOpts)`. Increments: `f.slice(-1e9, y)` shows the part below `y`; a liner is `DK.filling(tooth, prepOpts, 'gic').slice(floor, floor + 0.5)` with the composite `.slice(floor + 0.5)` above it. Hide the prep void once the last increment is flush. To make increments read, build one `DK.filling` per increment, each sliced to its own height range in two close shades (its own section group each), and pulse a `DK.curingLight` over each. |
| `DK.sealant(tooth, {path:[[xn,zn],…], width, thickness})` → mesh | A resin lens flowed along a groove (normalised crown coordinates; default the central groove), filling the fissure to its rims plus `thickness` mm (0.2). |
| `DK.matrixBand(tooth, {side, height, bottom, at, gap, thickness, sweep})` → mesh | Sectional steel band wrapped round the proximal surface at the contact height (`at`, default the crest of curvature), from `bottom` up `height` mm. Keep it out of the section groups. In a cut figure, clip it with the section (`m.material.clippingPlanes = [fig.section.plane]` on a cloned material after `enableSection`), or its front half hides the box being filled. |
| `DK.wedge({length, height, width, taper})` → mesh | Interproximal wedge (triangular prism, base gingival), axis along z, inserted from the buccal: place it at `x = MD/2 + 0.7`, `y` just under the gingival floor. |

**Pitfalls (caries):** the lesion's `set()` rebuilds four small meshes; keep it to one `fig.param`. Section order for a lesion: … enamel, dentin, `enamel` lesion (kind `'enamel'`), `affected` (kind `'dentin'`), pulp, `reactionary` (`'dentin'`), `infected` (`'plain'`), `cavity` (`color: DK.holeCap(fig)`, `'plain'`), then the prep (same colour) and the filling increments (`DK.COLORS.compositeCap` and `DK.COLORS.compositeLight` alternating, `'plain'`: muted ungrained grey-green that reads as a restoration and sets each increment apart). Anchor lesion labels with `g.point(...)` at `k = 0` or `12` so they lie in a mesio-distal cut plane. A prep's `set()` after `slice()` loses the slice; fillings are built once and only sliced. The matrix band is a vertical strip fitted at one height: with a cut through its centre it shows as a line, so view it from a three-quarter angle.
