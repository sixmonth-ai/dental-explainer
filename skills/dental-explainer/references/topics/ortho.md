# Orthodontics: brackets, archwires, aligners, tooth movement

Facts: `references/dental-facts.md` §19. Read it before planning.

## Building a figure

Read dental-facts.md §9 and §19 first (slot and wire sizes are in **inches**, forces in grams, the PDL must be exaggerated to be seen).
- **Fixed appliance, upper arch** (brackets, tubes, ligatures, archwire, power chain): copy `examples/braces-upper-arch.js`. Teeth from `DK.arch({ jaw: 'maxilla', placement: 'jaw', teeth: [16…26] })` (crown-down in the mouth, left teeth mirrored), `DK.gingivaArch(arch)` for the ridge, `DK.bracket` at the MBT-style heights of §19 (upper: 5.0 / 4.5 / 5.0 / 4.5 / 4.0, tube 3.5) with `DK.ligature`, `DK.molarTube` on the first molars, `DK.archwire` through each part's `wirePoint()`; parts explode along each tooth's outward normal (`slot.n`), the wire forward. Hero camera at three-quarter azimuth (`theta` ≈ 0.68), **below** the arch (`phi` ≈ 1.98) to reveal its curve. Say the slot and wire size in the subtitle; call heights "MBT-style", never universal.
- **Fixed appliance, lower arch or a segment** ("braces on the lower front teeth"): copy `examples/braces-lower-anterior.js` (43–33, aligning stage with a 0.014 in NiTi). `DK.arch({ jaw: 'mandible', teeth: [43, 42, 41, 31, 32, 33] })` (crowns up, no placement needed), lower heights from §19 (4.0 / 4.0 / 5.0 / 4.0 / 4.0, tube 3.5), the same `DK.gingivaArch(arch)` (it puts the ridge **below** the crowns and the scalloped margin on its upper face), hero camera at three-quarter azimuth (`theta` ≈ 0.52), **above** the teeth (`phi` ≈ 1.20). Lower incisors slope ~20–25°: `DK.bracket` stands square to the surface normal, so nothing floats.
- A single tooth with a bracket: build it in the tooth frame and label slot, wings, base. Anchor the **Archwire** label at a wire point between two brackets (the midpoint of two `wirePoint()`s), never inside a bracket (the label dims).
- **Retainers and relapse** (why a retainer, what happens without one): copy `examples/retainer-why.js`. One arch posed between crowded and straight with `fig.param`, ghosts at the crowded positions, then a split into a no-retainer copy that relapses partway (facts §19, Retention and relapse) and a copy with the bonded lingual wire 33–43.
- **Tooth movement** (tipping vs bodily, pressure and tension sides, remodelling): copy `examples/tooth-movement-11.js`. A procedure of 4 steps (force → tipping → bodily → bone remodels) on a labio-lingual cut, the socket (`DK.pdl`) fixed while `DK.moveTooth` moves the tooth about `DK.centreOfResistance`; accent only on the pressure side. Subtitle must say "PDL width exaggerated".
- **Aligners**: copy `examples/aligner-upper-arch.js`. One `DK.alignerArch` shell over the arch (0.5 mm, cast from a spine through the crowns), `DK.attachment` with the bevel that matches the movement (gingival bevel = extrusion); `DK.aligner` for a single tooth. The cut through an incisor is the teaching shot. Never show an aligner without attachments when the movement needs them.
- Watch for: brackets "at the gum line"; rectangular steel as a first wire; a single force drawn as bodily movement; bone "pushed aside"; slot sizes quoted in mm.

## Kit API

Appliance parts are built in the **tooth frame** (CEJ y = 0, +x mesial, +z labial; gingival = −y) and seat exactly on the crown surface of the preset, so they follow the tooth's transform (mirroring, arch rotation). Inch sizes are converted for you.

| call | returns |
|---|---|
| `DK.SLOTS['0.022' \| '0.018']` | `{w, d, name}`: slot height and depth in mm (0.559 × 0.711; 0.457 × 0.635). |
| `DK.wireSize('0.014' \| '0.019x0.025' \| {h, d})` | `{h, d, round, name}` in mm: `h` is the occluso-gingival height, `d` the labio-lingual depth. |
| `DK.crownSurface(spec, side=1).at(x, y)` | `{p:[x,y,z], n:[nx,ny,nz]}` on the facial (`side` −1: lingual) crown surface at MD offset `x` and height `y` above the CEJ. |
| `DK.bracket(fdiOrSpec, {slot:'0.022', height, x, width, tall:3, pad:0.3, material:'steel'\|'ceramic'})` → group | Twin bracket with rounded steel edges and recessed tie-wing necks, **square to the crown surface**: the group's z axis is the surface normal at the FA point and its y axis the tooth's long axis, the bonding base is contoured between the crown and the body's flat back. **The group's origin is the slot centre**; `height` is the slot centre from the incisal edge / cusp tip (default mid-crown = Andrews' FA point; MBT-style values in dental-facts §19). `grp.wirePoint(wireDepthMm)` → the wire's centre line point (wire bottomed in the slot) **in the frame of the group's outermost ancestor**: the tooth frame while the bracket is loose or a child of the tooth group, the world once that group is in the scene (it calls `updateWorldMatrix` first, so it honours any transform applied to the bracket). `userData: {slot, width, tall, floor, face, normal, height, frame}` (floor / face are local z offsets from the slot centre). |
| `DK.gingivaArch(arch, {height:3.4, depth:3.2, molarExtra:1.2, round:0.45, rise:1.6, eminence:0.22, material})` → mesh | Gingiva for a `DK.arch`: a thin ridge from the distal of the first tooth to the distal of the last, placed **above crown-down teeth (maxilla, placement 'jaw') or below crown-up teeth (mandible)**; the free margin follows the cervical outlines, with `rise` mm of interdental papilla above the 0.55 mm mid-facial margin and low root eminences. The narrower coronal lip blends into a modest attached band. Closed solid with end caps and corrected winding for mirrored jaws, baked into world coordinates; add it with `fit: false`. Call it after the arch is in the scene. |
| `DK.molarTube(fdiOrSpec, {slot, height, length:4, hook:true, band:0})` → group | Buccal tube with gingival hook, same frame and `wirePoint()` as a bracket; `band: 0.15` seats it on a `DK.band` instead of a pad. |
| `DK.band(fdiOrSpec, {thickness:0.15, bottom:0.8, top})` → mesh | Steel band around the crown between the two heights. |
| `DK.ligature(bracket, {r:0.22, color})` → mesh | Elastomeric O-ring seated behind the wing caps, with lateral runs below the slot floor and outside the body, positioned like the bracket (add it to the same parent). |
| `DK.powerChain([brackets], {r, color})` → group | Rings joined by links, built in **world** coordinates from the brackets' current world transforms (place them first). |
| `DK.archwire([[x,y,z]…], {size:'0.019x0.025', material:'steel'\|'niti'\|'tma', overhang:1.5, up:[0,1,0]})` → mesh | Round or rectangular wire swept through the points (any frame; use each bracket's `wirePoint()` mapped to the world). Closed solid; `userData.curve` is the CatmullRom. |
| `DK.attachment(fdiOrSpec, {x, y, w:3, h:2, d:1, bevel:'gingival'\|'occlusal'\|'mesial'\|'distal'\|null, side:1})` → mesh | Composite aligner attachment on the facial surface; `bevel` names the side the thick ledge faces. `userData.spec` feeds `DK.aligner`'s `bumps`. |
| `DK.aligner(fdiOrSpec, {thickness:0.5, gap:0.05, bumps:[att.userData.spec…], opacity})` → mesh | Translucent closed shell over one crown, trimmed at the CEJ, bulging over the attachments. One shell per tooth; group them. Section-ready (`kind:'plain'`). |
| `DK.alignerArch(spineCurve, [enamel meshes, attachments], {thickness:0.5, gap, segments:150, around:44, sweep, trimY:-0.9, down:[0,-1,0], smooth:2, opacity:0.16})` → mesh | **One continuous aligner over a whole arch**, in world coordinates: rays from a spine curve through the middle of the crowns are cast around the arch against the crown meshes; the shell lies `thickness` outside the farthest hit (attachments included), bridges the contacts and is trimmed where it reaches `trimY` (the gingival margin). Casts against both sides of enamel and attachments, restores their materials, and smooths only outward to preserve clearance at sampled attachment hits. The glossy clear material includes two polished trim-edge child meshes; keep the default material (do not replace it with zirconia). Transparent shell and edges do not cast opaque shadows. Higher sampling (the example uses `segments:220, around:72`) costs more build time. Prefer it to per-tooth `DK.aligner` shells for an arch. `down` is the direction of the incisal edges (`[0,1,0]` for crowns-up teeth, then `trimY` is the margin height). |
| `DK.sweepGeometry(curve, (s, t, {P, U, N}) => [a, b], …)` | The profile callback also receives the position `t` along the curve and the frame, so a sweep can vary along the arch: the arch examples drop the ridge's lower face into a free gingival margin around each neck and raise eminences over the roots. |
| `DK.centreOfResistance(spec, crestY, fraction=0.4)` → y | Centre of resistance of a single-rooted tooth: `fraction` of the root below the crest. |
| `DK.moveTooth(group, {tip, pivotY, shift})` | Rotates the tooth `tip` rad about the x axis through `(0, pivotY, 0)` (negative tips the crown lingually) and shifts it `shift` mm along z. Keep the socket (`DK.pdl(spec, gap, crest)`) fixed in the world, so a cut shows the PDL compressed on one side and widened on the other; translate the socket afterwards to show remodelling. |
| `DK.ribbon(points, width, normal, {color, alpha, material, name})`, `DK.arrow({from, to, width, head, normal, color, alpha, material})` | Flat overlay markers (zones along a root, a force arrow) with exact colours; put them 0.1–0.2 mm in front of a cut face. |
| `DK.sweepGeometry(curve, profile, {segments, radial, closed, up})`, `DK.roundedRect(hw, hd, r)`, `DK.slabGeometry(nu, nv, fn, wrapU)` | Building blocks: a profile swept along a THREE curve (a gingival ridge along an arch: `DK.roundedRect(3, 5.6, 1.6)`), and a closed slab between an inner and an outer grid. |

Pitfalls:
- A regular `fig.part` resets its object's position every frame, so a tooth moved by `DK.moveTooth` must sit inside a holder group that is the part (see `examples/tooth-movement-11.js`).
- To explode brackets, ligatures or aligner shells separately, build them in the tooth frame, then `obj.updateMatrix(); obj.applyMatrix4(tooth.group.matrixWorld)` and register them as world parts (the braces and aligner examples do this).
- Per-tooth `DK.aligner` shells overlap at the contacts (doubled edges); use `DK.alignerArch` for an arch. With `DK.aligner`, pass the attachments' `userData.spec` as `bumps` or the shell cuts through them.
- Arch examples: place the teeth with `DK.arch` (register `arch.group` as the regular part, teeth `inPlace`), then build the appliance parts in each tooth's frame, read `wirePoint()` while the part is still loose (tooth frame) and map it with `tooth.group.localToWorld`, then move the part into the world with `obj.updateMatrix(); obj.applyMatrix4(tooth.group.matrixWorld)` (left teeth are mirrored, which the decomposed scale carries). `DK.gingivaArch(arch)` draws the ridge; `view.margin` ≈ 0.68 keeps 16–26 in frame with some air, and the hero view is a three-quarter shot from below (`theta` 0.68, `phi` 1.98) for a maxillary arch, above (`theta` ≈ 0.52, `phi` ≈ 1.20) for a mandibular one. Image mode frames the same shot (no per-mode zoom needed).
- Inside the kit, keep topic helpers uniquely named: function declarations are hoisted across topic blocks, so two `function sweepGeometry` definitions silently replace each other (the ortho sweep is `orthoSweep`, exported as `DK.sweepGeometry`).
- Crowns-only teeth (`DK.tooth(fdi, {omit:['dentin','pulp','root']})`) are hollow in section; for a cut through an arch keep full teeth and shorten `spec.roots[].len` so the roots stay inside the ridge.
